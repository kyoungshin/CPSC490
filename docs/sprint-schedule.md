# Sprint schedule — Fall 2026

> CPSC 490, Sections 01 (Tuesdays) and 05 (Thursdays), E 202 at normal class
> time. Course reference — maintained by the instructor; this file is excluded
> from the scaffold copy (`.gitattributes export-ignore`), so it does not land
> in team repositories.


This is the meeting schedule for the four sprints and the final
presentations. Each group has a **30-minute slot** with me at every sprint
review. Section 01 meets **Tuesdays**, Section 05 meets **Thursdays**, in
E 202 at our normal class time.

Two things before the calendar:

- **Come with your repository open.** The sprint review is a look at your
  board, your issues, and your proposal draft — not a presentation. If your
  board does not match what you tell me, the board is what I believe.
- **Groups 01 and 12 are cross-section.** If you are in one of those teams
  and your section is not the one your group is scheduled on, come on your
  **group's** night. Group 01 meets Tuesdays; Group 12 meets Thursdays.
